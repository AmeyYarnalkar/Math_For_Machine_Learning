## Steps to Find the Equation of a Tangent Line at a Given Point




### **1️. Make sure you know:**

* Function $ f(x) $
* Point where tangent is needed $ x=a $

If they give a point $(a,b)$, quickly verify $b=f(a)$.

---

### **2️. Differentiate the function**

Find the derivative:

$$
f'(x)
$$

This gives the **slope of the curve** at any $x$.

---

### **3️. Find slope at the given point**

Plug (x=a) into derivative:

$$
m = f'(a)
$$

This $m$ is the slope of the tangent line.

---

### **4️. Get the point on the curve**

Compute:

$$
y_0 = f(a)
$$

So your point is $(a,y_0)$.

---

### **5️. Use point–slope form of a line**

Tangent equation:

$$
y - y_0 = m(x - a)
$$

That’s the cleanest form.
You can expand if they want standard form.

---

##  Quick Example

Find tangent to $ f(x)=x^2 $ at $x=2$.

**Step 1 — Derivative**
$$
f'(x)=2x
$$

**Step 2 — Slope**
$$
m=f'(2)=4
$$

**Step 3 — Point**
$$
y_0=f(2)=4
$$
Point is $(2,4)$.

**Step 4 — Equation**
$$
y-4=4(x-2)
$$

Simplify:
$$
y=4x-4
$$
